3.2 Campaign-based installment plans

Eligible subscribers see an installment option at the point of purchase for campaigns flagged by the campaign owner.

The subscriber may split a campaign charge into 3, 6 or 12 installments; eligibility rules are defined by the campaign owner.

Installment lines appear on the monthly invoice under the campaign heading.

budget ≈ 45 person-days · go-live Nov 2026quarantined
